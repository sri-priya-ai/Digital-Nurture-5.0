Accessibility checklist covered in this hands-on:
- All inputs have associated <label> elements
- nav has aria-label="Main navigation"
- Active nav link has aria-current="page"
- Search result count uses role="status" aria-live="polite"
- Course cards are keyboard-focusable (tabindex=0) and respond to Enter key
- Visible focus outlines on links and cards (no outline:none used)
- Text color #1a1a1a on #ffffff background passes WCAG AA contrast (>4.5:1)
